## Outreach and mentoring

- **Sustainable Energy Activity Lab (SEAL)** (2023–2024). Mentor for high school clubs working on year-long sustainable energy projects involving microbial fuel cells, with weekly labs and discussions.
- **FUTURE Ignited** (2023). Peer advisor providing weekly guidance to underrepresented undergraduate students on graduate school applications.
- **First-year Success Research Institute (FSRI)** (2023). Summer research mentor for three first-year students from historically excluded backgrounds.
- **Muir High School** (2018–2020). Tutor in math and science through the Caltech Y Rise program.
- **Caltech Outreach** (2017–2018). Helped organize Pasadena Science and Art Night, Tech Savvy Science Day for Girls, and facilitated talks through the Caltech Center for Diversity.
- **Georgia Tech PLUS** (2013–2016). Peer-led study leader, developing interactive weekly review sessions for physics and calculus.
- **Georgia Tech Invention Studio** (2014–2016). Volunteer instructor on fabrication equipment; hosted STEM outreach workshops for local high schools.
